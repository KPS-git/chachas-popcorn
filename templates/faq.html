How fresh is the popcorn?

ChaCha’s popcorn should be packed with freshness in mind. For the best experience, customers should keep bags sealed until they are ready to enjoy them.

What flavors are available?

The initial lineup includes Original, Cheddar, and Caramel, with room for seasonal flavors and future limited drops.

Can customers build a bundle?

Yes. The site is designed around simple individual bag purchases and an easy starter box featuring all three core flavors.

How does checkout work?

The site is structured so Django can create a Stripe Checkout session. Stripe should handle payment securely, then return the customer to the confirmation page.

Can customers create an account?

Yes. The account pages are designed as a frontend shell so Django authentication can later manage login, saved addresses, and order history.
