Returns

Order support with care.

Because popcorn is a food product, return handling should be clear, careful, and customer-friendly.

Food product policy

Because ChaCha’s Popcorn is a consumable food product, returns may be limited once an item has shipped or been opened. Final policy language should be confirmed by ChaCha’s before launch.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If an order arrives damaged or incorrect, customers should contact ChaCha’s with their order number and photos so the team can review the issue.

Refund review

Refunds, replacements, or credits can be reviewed case by case based on the order issue and final store policy.

Need help?

Use the contact page for order support, damaged shipments, missing items, or questions about a recent purchase.
