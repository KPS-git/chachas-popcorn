Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

How ChaCha’s Popcorn may collect, use, and protect customer information.

Information we collect

ChaCha’s Popcorn may collect information customers provide when placing an order, creating an account, joining the email list, or contacting support. This may include name, email address, phone number, shipping address, order details, and customer support messages.

How information is used

Customer information may be used to process orders, provide shipping updates, respond to support requests, improve the shopping experience, and send marketing emails when customers choose to subscribe.

Payments

Payment details should be processed securely through Stripe or another approved payment provider. ChaCha’s Popcorn should not directly store full credit card numbers on this website.

Email marketing

Customers who subscribe may receive occasional emails about product drops, limited releases, exclusive offers, and ChaCha’s Popcorn updates. Customers may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in marketing emails.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies to understand site performance, improve customer experience, and support marketing efforts. A cookie banner may be added before launch if needed.

Information sharing

ChaCha’s Popcorn may share limited information with trusted service providers needed to operate the store, such as payment processors, shipping providers, email platforms, analytics tools, and website hosting services.

Data security

ChaCha’s Popcorn should use reasonable safeguards to protect customer information. No online system is perfect, but the site should be built with secure payment processing and responsible data handling in mind.

Customer choices

Customers may contact ChaCha’s Popcorn to ask questions about their information, update account details, or request support related to their order or communication preferences.
